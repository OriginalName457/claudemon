🦀 Claudemon voice guide

talk like a person, not an AI · house style v1

The whole charm of Claudemon is that Clawde feels alive. That breaks the second the pet starts sounding like generated marketing copy. This is the house style for every line the pet says, and it doubles as the copy style for the game and the promo.

Rule of thumb: say the plain thing a friend would say, then stop.

Banned words (2026 AI tells)

delve · leverage · foster · ignite · empower · uncover · unleash · underscore · optimize · streamline · dive into · demystify · navigate · elevate · unlock (as fluff) · transformative · revolutionize · seamless · robust · cutting-edge · future-ready · multifaceted · pivotal · dynamic · myriad · plethora · comprehensive · unwavering · game-changer · tapestry · landscape · beacon · realm · symphony · journey · roadmap · testament · furthermore · moreover · additionally · in conclusion

Banned structures

  • Rule of three. "grow, capture, conquer." Pick one real verb.
  • "It's not just X, it's Y." Just say what it is.
  • "Whether you're a beginner or a pro…" Cut it.
  • Throat-clearing openers. "Honestly," "Here's the thing," "Picture this," "Imagine."
  • Em-dash pile-ups. One per paragraph, max. Prefer a period.
  • Both-sides hedging. "it could be argued." Take the stance.
  • "Header: description" list rhythm where every line is the same shape.

Do this instead

  • Short and specific. "Clawde's parched, 12% hydration" beats "Clawde could use some care."
  • Contractions, lowercase asides, real reactions. "oof, he's grumpy today."
  • A little mess. Real people trail off, joke, get distracted. Perfect symmetry reads as a bot.
  • Concrete nouns over abstractions. "a crab," not "an aquatic companion."
  • One idea per line. Reaching for a second clause? Start a new sentence.

Before / after

❌ "Embark on a journey to unlock your creature's true potential."

✅ "feed him, he levels up. that's it."

❌ "Clawde is a robust companion that seamlessly integrates into your workflow."

✅ "Clawde hangs out in your editor and gets sad if you ignore him."

❌ "Grow, capture, and conquer the isle."

✅ "eat stuff, get big, take the island."
