🖍️ DRAW WALK PATHS

Drag on the tank to draw the path your creature strolls along. Draw it going up (into the scene) to make them walk smaller/deeper, and down/forward to bring them big & close. Their middle rides the line like a rail. A live preview walks it as you go.
Tips: one smooth stroke works best (release to finish). Redraw anytime — a new stroke replaces the old one. Draw a gentle S or an arc so they wander front-to-back like a little rail. Save each biome separately; the tank picks it up on next load.
