BIOME OVERHAUL

vision board · base tanks & upgrade tiers · nothing built yet — react & steer
The idea: each biome becomes a real little scene your creatures live in — a painted pixel background with a clear ground line they walk along, gentle motion (ocean current, drifting pollen, heat shimmer), and layered props you buy in the shop. Tank upgrades don't just make the box bigger — they make the world richer: more life, more depth, more to look at.

How it renders: a background image per biome + tier → creatures walk the ground line → a light animated flow layer on top → your purchased decorations layered in front. The old code-drawn floor/decor gets fully replaced with real pixel art like the samples below.
