PIXELLAB vs RECRAFT

same creature, two pipelines — this is the choice before we commit

🦀 PixelLab

CONSISTENT · REAL ANIMATION · 1 gen each
Actually walks (4-frame cycle, real generated frames), gives 4 directions, and every form will share this exact style. Trade-off: lower detail — classic small-sprite pixel art (~64px).

✨ Recraft (current)

DETAILED · STATIC · inconsistent line
one pose only · no directions · no frames
Gorgeous chunky detail (the one you loved), but it's a single static pose, only faked motion, and each evolution drifts in style.
The call: PixelLab = a cohesive line that truly animates, at lower detail · Recraft = higher detail, but static & style-inconsistent.
Next options: keep PixelLab as-is · push PixelLab detail higher (128px / reference the Recraft look) · go back to Recraft.
